# Product Context - RoobsWorld

## Product Summary

RoobsWorld is a Spore-inspired evolution experience focused on the first two stages of progression:

- Cell stage
- Creature stage

The product direction is depth and polish in these two stages instead of breadth across all classic Spore stages.

## Player Value

- Fast, readable survival loop in Cell stage.
- Strong expression and identity through creature customization.
- Clear progression from microscopic survival to creature embodiment.

## UX Priorities

1. Immediate fun in the first session.
2. Smooth onboarding through tutorial prompts.
3. Clear goals and rewards (DNA, progression, achievements).
4. Reliable save/load and session continuity.

## Platform Context

Target platforms remain PC and consoles, with performance and control consistency as a release requirement.

## Out of Scope

- Additional stage gameplay loops beyond Cell and Creature
